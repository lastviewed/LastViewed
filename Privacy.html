Privacy Policy

Last Viewed — Closed Circuit Custody Vault

Effective: 30 August 2026 · YPS Services LLC (California, B20250292295)

This policy applies to the Last Viewed software, the Google Play listing when published, and this website. It describes what YPS Services LLC (“YPS”, “we”) does and does not collect.

1. Short version

  • Credentials are stored on your device.
  • YPS does not collect them.
  • They are not shared with YPS or third parties through this product.
  • They are not transmitted for sync. There is no YPS cloud and no vendor account.
  • Clipboard use, when you choose it, stays on the local device.

2. No YPS account

Last Viewed does not require a Google, Apple, or YPS sign-in to use the vault. We do not operate a login service for vault contents. We cannot see, recover, or reset your master password or vault from our side.

3. Data stored on your device

The application creates local files on the device you choose, including an encrypted store, a rolling backup, key-derivation material, a device-binding file, and local settings. These files stay on that device unless you copy them yourself.

Last Viewed metadata (which credential was revealed, and when) is stored in the local vault as part of the product. That log does not leave the device through YPS servers.

4. Data we do not collect

YPS does not collect:

  • passwords, usernames, TOTP secrets, or Last Viewed logs
  • your master password
  • contacts, photos, location, or microphone/camera data as part of this product
  • analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports from the vault itself unless a future build discloses that separately in this policy and in Play Data safety

If a distribution channel (for example Google Play) collects installer or store-account data, that is Google’s processing under Google’s policies, not YPS vault processing.

5. Website

This website is static documentation. We do not run an account system on it. Your web host or DNS provider may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request time) according to their own terms. YPS does not use this site to profile vault users.

If you email support@ypsservices.app, we receive whatever you send in that message so we can reply. Do not email vault contents, master passwords, or seed material.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share vault data, because we do not hold it. We may share contact details you email us if required by law or to enforce our EULA.

7. Children

Last Viewed is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Encryption and device binding run on your device. You are responsible for the machine, the master password, physical custody of backups, and any USB or air-gapped copy you make. YPS cannot decrypt a vault we never received.

9. Retention

Vault data remains until you delete the local files. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the request or as required by law.

10. Your choices

Uninstall the app and delete local vault files to remove product data from a device. There is no YPS cloud account to delete. If you emailed us, write to support@ypsservices.app to ask us to delete that correspondence.

11. California / similar laws

Because YPS does not collect vault contents, there is no YPS-held credential file to access or delete. For emails you sent us, you may request access or deletion at support@ypsservices.app.

12. International users

YPS Services LLC is organized in California, United States. This site and support email are operated from that business. The product is designed so vault contents do not cross to YPS.

13. Changes

If the product begins collecting any data, this page will be updated before that behavior ships, and Play Data safety will be updated to match. The date at the top will change.
